Input parameters

Seven Key Agricultural Parameters

Each signal helps describe the conditions that influence crop suitability.

N

Nitrogen

Supports leaf growth and chlorophyll production. Range: 0-200 mg/kg.

P

Phosphorus

Supports root development and energy transfer. Range: 0-200 mg/kg.

K

Potassium

Regulates water uptake and disease resistance. Range: 0-200 mg/kg.

🌡

Temperature

Describes ambient conditions affecting crop growth rate.

💧

Humidity

Represents moisture conditions and transpiration pressure.

pH

Soil pH

Indicates acidity and nutrient availability. Range: 0-14.

🌧

Rainfall

Shows annual water availability and irrigation needs.
