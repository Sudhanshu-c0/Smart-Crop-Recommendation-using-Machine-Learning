📚 Fertilizer management best practices

Guidelines for sustainable nutrient management and soil preservation

⚖️

Balanced nutrient ratios

Avoid over-application of single nutrients. Balanced NPK ratios help prevent fertilizer runoff and groundwater contamination.

⏳

Timing and split application

Apply fertilizer during key growth stages rather than all at once to improve nutrient uptake efficiency.

🍂

Combine with organic matter

Compost, green manure, and farmyard manure improve soil health and fertilizer retention capacity.
